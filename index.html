05 A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
head for the hills.

Some quick notes about Ghumketu and the kind of stays we believe in.

What is Ghumketu? +

Ghumketu is a platform being created to help travellers discover curated, locally run homestays and meaningful local experiences, starting with the quieter corners of Shimla and Himachal.

What kind of homestays will I find? +

Expect characterful homes hosted by local people, where the welcome, regional food and connection to the landscape matter more than hotel-style facilities.

Are there outdoor stays in Shimla? +

Ghumketu is focused on peaceful stays close to nature and local life around Shimla. Stay listings and booking details will be shared as the platform develops.

What makes a Ghumketu homestay different? +

Ghumketu focuses on curated, locally run homestays around Shimla where guests can experience regional food, traditions and personal hospitality. The idea is to help travellers find a stay with a local connection, not just a room for the night.

Can I book a homestay in Shimla through Ghumketu today? +

Ghumketu is currently being developed. Listings and booking information for Shimla homestays will be added as the platform takes shape.

Is Ghumketu only for Shimla? +

Shimla and Himachal are where the story begins. The idea is to help people find hidden homestays and welcoming hosts in more places over time.
